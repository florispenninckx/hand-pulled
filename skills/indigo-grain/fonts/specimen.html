The face

Sunprint is laid on cyanotype paper and exposed. At EXPO 0 it is a crisp contact print; as the exposure runs, light creeps under the edges, the strokes swell and pool, and the edge wanders like paper fibre. Sunprint Halo is the light that got under the letters: set it behind the core, and its glow and grain follow the same advances at every exposure.
