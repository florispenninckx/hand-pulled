Specula SHEN 0–1000 · variable · 65 KB

A wide techno face with an inline: a channel of light cut along every stroke. At SHEN 0 it is a hairline glint; as SHEN rises the channel widens and slides toward the light until each stroke is a polished rim around a bright core.

Contrail TRAL 0–1000 · variable · 82 KB

A heavy italic dragged through the light: each letter keeps a crisp leading edge while every row behind it is pulled back into a streak, longer or shorter row by row, the same rows all along the line, like paint dragged across charcoal or a light in a long exposure. TRAL sets how far it has travelled.

Fluted REED 0–1000 · variable · 83 KB

A fat round display face behind fluted glass: the letter is read through vertical reeds. At REED 0 the glass is flat and the letter whole; as REED rises each reed narrows what it shows to a sliver and bends it sideways, so the letter breaks into bright bars.

Pool RIPL 0–1000 · variable · 97 KB

A rounded techno face standing on liquid metal: under the baseline each letter has its own mirror image, cut into bands that thin as they sink. At RIPL 0 the pool is still; as RIPL rises the bands narrow to slivers and ripple sideways, as a strip light does on wet chrome.
