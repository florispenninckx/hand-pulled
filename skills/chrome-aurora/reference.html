The wall, poured and still moving

The first pour again, live. A lamp follows your hand across the chrome, a click drops a ripple into it, the aurora drifts over its highlights and scrolling tilts the room it reflects. Frame zero is the still.

Houselights, the desk

A small made-up lighting desk for the hall, built only from the live pieces: cue cards that rise out of the black, chrome buttons, switches, a master fader, a fade bar, a syncing bead, and one chrome focus ring for the whole page (press Tab).

Four nights, four posters

Each night has its own surface. A trail dragged across charcoal, a swirl of cyan with one red tongue in it, black chrome turning round an eye, and a slab of amber light falling into blue. The posters are the plates themselves, with the type small and pushed into the corners.

The wall, pass by pass

The opening pour taken apart. A height field from warped noise, packed tight at the rim like a meniscus. Its slope gives a normal. The normal reflects a studio: a dark ceiling, a warm lamp on one side, a cool lamp opposite. Each colour channel bends a little differently, which is where the fringes come from. Grain goes on last. Pour the wall again and these follow it.

The other looks

The same five surfaces with other lamps. Drop a photograph on any of them: its light and dark become the height of the liquid, so a face or a window turns into a pour that still reads as the picture.

Lamps, and the four nights

The hall is black. Every colour on these plates is a lamp, a film or a fringe; the page itself keeps to black, one warm grey and a single hot orange.
