Late listening in the annealing hallKiln
Hours

Six Friday nights in the hall where the day's glass cools. One record a night, played from the first groove to the last, while the lehr brings the work down from five hundred degrees. Each night has its own poster. Every poster is soft colour behind a surface: fluted glass, a signal dragged sideways, silk folds, grain.

Nights
six Fridays, 6 November to 11 December 2026
Hours
21:00 until the lehr is cold
Hall
the annealing hall, forty cushions, the lights at a tenth
Posters
one per night, each seen through a different pane

+ Drop a photograph on any poster and it becomes that poster's light. The pane stays; the colour is yours.

+Six nights

A poster for every Friday. Each one is a single colour field and one surface. Nothing is drawn on top except the words, and those stay small and sharp, because a rough surface under clean type is the whole look.

+One field, four panes

The season poster again, without its words. First the bare colour field, then that same field seen through three different surfaces. The colour stays the same; only the surface changes. If you pull the season poster again, these four follow it.

+The pane library

Every surface the house keeps, one poster per kind. Choose a colourway and the poster is pulled again with the same seed, so the folds, the flowers and the tears land where they were.

+Colours and Fridays

Each colourway runs dark to light, and grain sits over all of it. The only flat colour on these posters is in the type.

  1. 6 NovAfterglow. The season opens at 21:00, with the first record played while the lehr is still hot.
  2. 13 NovSlow Signal. A shortwave set by Maren Oyelaran, tuned live, all static and drift.
  3. 20 NovAnneal. One long piece for bowed glass, played by Teodor Vasz while the room slowly cools.
  4. 27 NovThe Coordinate. Field recordings from the quarry, played on four speakers placed on a grid.
  5. 4 DecRose Cullet. Records brought by the glassblowers, one side each, talk allowed between sides.
  6. 11 DecFirst Light. The last night. It runs until dawn, and there is coffee at the loading door.
