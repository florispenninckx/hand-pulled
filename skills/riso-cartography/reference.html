An atlas of Stavmere in seven pullsFigure & Ground

Stavmere drawn the way old town plans were drawn: every block that someone owns is ink, and everything the town shares is paper. That means the streets, the squares, the quays and the river Wenn. None of the plans was traced. Each street grew along a field of directions laid down by the river and the old square. The river moved its bends for two hundred years before it was printed. Then everything went through the duplicator one drum at a time.

Pull sheet I again for a new town, or change its ink. Drop a photograph on it and the town prints the picture: each block takes the tone underneath it.

The same ground, three more ways

Figure and ground only asks which side of the street a mark is on. The rest is the printer's choice. A wash dries darker at its edges. Footprints show the houses a block is made of. A hatch gives each block its own hand. Each of these sheets is a new town from the same rules.

The river, the land, the walk

Out of town the paper opens up. The Wenn is shown with every bed it has left behind. Next comes a land-use plan in the pastel washes planners use, with its proposals as hollow arrows. Last is a poster for the club's spring walk, huge black words over a green valley.

Sheet VI, drum by drum

The land-use sheet again, one stencil at a time. Each drum starts as a master drawn in black. The press screens it into grain or a halftone of dots, lands it a fraction of a millimetre off, and lays it over whatever is already on the paper. Pink under purple gives the violet of the works. Every word sits in a box of bare paper, cut out of all three drums. Pull sheet VI again and these follow it.

The Drum Room

The club's press queue, built from the live pieces. The map behind it is printed in blue on two drums: a grain tint and the contours of the same ground. The drums wander out of register and back, and a fresh hit of ink traces the contours one level at a time. Point at the map for a loupe and click to stamp it. Every control here is a native one, with the print behind it.
