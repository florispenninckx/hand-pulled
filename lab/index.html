Twelve new display faces for indigo, cartography and abstract

Four candidates per style, each a different base and a different process, built by tools/foundry.py from SIL OFL bases. Open a style to see its current face first, then the four candidates, each on a poster and a site hero over the style's live engine, with its axis slider. Pick keep, maybe or drop; your picks stay in this browser. When you are done, copy the verdict and paste it back.

The bar: what the BOC faces do right

  1. One strong idea per face, named by the process: Soak bleeds, Globule melts, Flatbed flattens, Gouge carves.
  2. A characterful heavy base with room for the effect: the process needs mass to act on.
  3. The process is visible at display size and the word still reads at every axis value.
  4. The axis runs from a clean letter to the full effect, so one face covers a quiet heading and a loud poster word.
  5. The attitude matches the board: materials, not decorations. Each candidate takes its material from a pin on the style's board (see NOTES.md).
