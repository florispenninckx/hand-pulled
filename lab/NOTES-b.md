# Fonts wave 3, lane B: notes

## What the BOC faces do right (Soak, Globule, Flatbed, Gouge)

1. The process changes the silhouette, not a detail inside it: bleed, drops, squash and crescent all read from across a room and still read at 30 px in the alphabet line.
2. Each face is one physical cause you can say in one word (soak, pour, press, carve), and the axis is how much of it happened; no filter is stacked on another filter.
3. The bases already have a build (black grotesk, wide techno, super-extended, fat round display), and the process pushes that build further instead of fighting it.
4. The curves are clean: spline-fitted, no pixel stairs or noise fuzz at display size, so the effect looks drawn by hand rather than rendered.
5. The whole axis is usable: the default already shows the idea, the two ends are "clean" and "full", and a line of real copy stays legible.

By that bar the current faces of this lane fall short in one way each: Rowdrag at SORT 0 is a plain pixel face, Lull mostly gets bolder as it "blooms", and Specula's inline is a hairline detail inside an ordinary techno letter.

## Lettering on the boards

- Pixel glitch: little lettering. 14 is a grotesk torn and doubled by a scanner, words stuttering over themselves; 19 is heavy grotesk in blue ink sliced by vertical drags and doubled rows. The images add vertical drips (33, 34, 41, 46), macroblocks (16, 20, 35) and a cracked screen (6).
- Ethereal haze: thin flared serifs (38 "BLUME", Friz-like caps; 39 "porte", a light serif with one cut stroke), a giant blurred glass "g" (37), stipple dots going soft (34), a meadow smeared by the shutter (19), ribbons turning over (36, 41).
- Chrome aurora: almost no type; tiny mono and serif captions (26). The display face has to come from the surfaces: thin-film fringes (11, 19), liquid pools and reflections (1, 21), paint trails with a crisp front and smeared tail (7, 11).
